    This page features a ChatBot that users can interact with. In addition to chatting with the bot, users can apply various techniques aimed at modifying the LLM's behavior at will, potentially inducing misalignment. "Alignment" in LLMs refers to the model's ability to respond in a way that is consistent with the user's intentions, avoiding undesired or harmful outputs. Using the tools available on this site, users can experiment with different "steering" (manipulation) techniques to observe how these influence the model's responses, and gain a deeper understanding of how LLMs work and what misalignment entails.

    Techniques

    Among the various techniques used to misalign the model:

    • Token Substitution: The manipulation of text generated by the model. The importance of this technique lies in the fact that past tokens always influence the probability of generating subsequent tokens, thus allowing the conversation to be steered at will.
    • Prefix Injection: Forcing the model to respond with a prefix, i.e. a series of tokens that can significantly influence the continuation of text generation.
    • Steering Vector: Vectors that allow modification of the model's internal states. In this way, developers can control and alter the model's behavior without modifying the underlying weights. The degree to which these vectors influence the LLM's behavior is determined by the multiplier, a value ranging from -1 to 1.
    • Temperature: A value ranging from 0 to 2 that determines the randomness and "creativity" of the responses generated by the model.

    Technical details

    The model runs on the cluster of the University of Milano-Bicocca, a private computing infrastructure. The cluster provides an access node with high storage capacity for datasets and research results (over 100 TeraBytes). The system is equipped with a compute node featuring 4 Nvidia L40S GPUs (up to 91.6 TFLOPS each) and 768 GB of RAM.

    The models used are Qwen2.5, ranging in size from 7B to 14B parameters. Qwen is a model developed by Alibaba Cloud. The model was trained on a multilingual and multi-domain dataset.
